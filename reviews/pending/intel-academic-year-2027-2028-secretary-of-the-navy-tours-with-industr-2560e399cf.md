### Summary

Eligibility: (1) All active-duty E6-E9 Sailors. (2) Training and Administration of the Reserves (TAR) Sailors are eligible for the SNTWI program. (3) Sailors must have a minimum of 5 years of service as of 1 September 2027. (4) SNTWI participants must have distinguished themselves by their performance in their fields and have high potential for future leadership positions. Applicants must meet physical readiness standards. (5) Sailors in receipt of orders, or within three years of their projected separation date, are not eligible. b.

### Details

This NAVADMIN announces the Academic Year (AY) 2027-2028 Secretary of the Navy Tours with Industry (SNTWI) program for Enlisted Sailors, per reference (a). This program is a unique and non-traditional opportunity for exceptional Sailors to work with partnering corporations. SNTWI fellows are immersed in industry to learn and observe corporate processes and leading practices.

Timeline. All AY27-28 applications are due to OPNAV N1-74 (Navy Education Branch) no later than 2359 EST Monday 11 January 2027. a. A selection panel will convene in January 2027. b.

Application process. Send requests for application templates and any questions to the Navy Fellowships Distro listed below. a. Applications require a Secretary of the Navy Tours with Industry Program Application (NAVPERS 1330/4), a curriculum vitae, biography, and endorsement letters from both your commanding officer and detailer. b.

Points of Contact. a. Application submission and program-specific questions: navy.fellowships@us.navy.mil b. Community-specific eligibility questions: please contact your detailer.
